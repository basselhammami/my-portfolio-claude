Designing for the people who protect people's money

CLM Platform RAKBANK

Senior Product Designer · Oct 2024 – Present · Web

Led the end-to-end redesign of RAKBANK's Customer Lifecycle Management platform — from a fragmented legacy system to a unified analyst-first tool used by the entire compliance function

Where it all started

When I joined RAKBANK, there was no unified tool for managing customer due diligence. The compliance team was navigating between 6+ disconnected screens, each built at a different time, by a different team.

People had come to accept the friction. Opening multiple tabs for a single customer review was just part of the job.

So I set out to change that.

The goal was simple: make compliance review feel clear, structured, and in control.

At the beginning, it was just me — one designer, a fragmented system, and 900K+ customers whose data was spread across it.

What I was solving for

We wanted to build a platform that felt like a precision tool built for experts — not a legacy database interface.

Why do I need 6 tabs just to review one customer?
Where did this customer's risk rating change?

The main design challenges were

  • Build clarity from complexity — no hidden data, no guesswork
  • Create a single unified view across fragmented data sources
  • Make compliance feel like expert control, not bureaucratic overhead
  • Scale for a team of 100+ analysts without losing the human touch

How I approached it

I mapped the CLM platform from the ground up — every screen, every state, every edge case.

There was no existing design pattern for this kind of compliance tool inside RAKBANK, so I started by spending weeks understanding the real workflows before touching any design tool.

  • 12 stakeholder interviews + 3 weeks of workflow observation
  • Full IA audit of 3 legacy systems to find consolidation opportunities
  • Mapped every data object and relationship before designing screens
  • Established a shared design system pattern library first
  • Shipped in phases — validated each module with analysts before moving to the next
  • Collaborated daily with PMs, engineers, and compliance officers

Design that changed behavior

The key was to make compliance feel like expert control — not bureaucratic overhead.

Analysts don't read data dictionaries. They look for signals of clarity, structure, and trust. So instead of exposing every raw data field, we gave them context and decisions that made sense.

  • Single-page customer profile replaced a 6-screen workflow
  • Context-aware action panel — showed only what was relevant for each case state
  • Inline document preview kept analysts in flow, no external PDF viewer needed
  • Risk timeline made data provenance visible — analysts finally trusted the data
  • Role-based views for analysts, compliance officers, and operations managers

What happened next

The new CLM platform became the primary compliance tool across RAKBANK.

↓ ~60% Reduction in time to complete a full customer review
3 → 1 Legacy systems consolidated into one platform
1-page Customer profile replacing a 6-screen workflow
900K+ Customers managed through the platform

Looking back

What started as a UX cleanup turned into a platform that changed how the entire compliance team worked.

The CLM platform didn't just improve the interface — it changed how analysts felt about their work. From frustrated and scattered, to in control.

It taught me that great enterprise design isn't about building feature-complete tools — it's about trust, clarity, and giving experts the right context at the right moment.
